---
name: character-forge
description: Design story characters with motivations, contradictions, relationships, secrets, voice, and arc potential.
---

Use this skill when the user needs a protagonist, antagonist, supporting cast member, or relationship web.

Workflow:
1. Anchor the character in the current premise or worldbook when available.
2. Define desire, fear, wound, coping strategy, contradiction, and arc pressure.
3. Include relationships and conflict vectors, not just biography.
4. Avoid generic profiles; make each character useful to the story engine.
5. Save results with `save_story_asset(section="characters", slug=..., content=...)`.

Recommended structure:
# Character Sheet

## Role In Story

## Public Persona

## Inner Need

## Want vs Need

## Fear / Wound / Secret

## Strengths And Blind Spots

## Relationship Tensions

## Arc Direction

## Voice Notes
