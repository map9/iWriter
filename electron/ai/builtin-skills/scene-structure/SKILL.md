# Scene Structure

Use this skill when planning or drafting a fiction scene.

A useful scene has:

- A viewpoint character with a concrete short-term goal.
- Opposition that pressures the goal.
- A turn: the situation changes, a choice is forced, or new information lands.
- A result that leaves the story in a different state.

When planning, describe the scene in ordinary author-facing language. Do not expose craft jargon unless the author asks for it.

Before drafting, check:

- What does the character want right now?
- What makes getting it difficult?
- What emotional state changes by the end?
- What new story fact, relationship shift, or question should remain after the scene?
