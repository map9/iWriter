# Character Behavior Check

Use this skill before reviewing a chapter for characterization consistency.

## Review Focus

- Compare actions and dialogue against known goals, fears, relationships, and prior choices.
- Distinguish growth under pressure from unmotivated personality drift.
- Check whether emotional reactions match the character's knowledge and stakes.
- Watch for voice flattening where different characters speak alike.

## Finding Guidance

- Mark minor issues when a line or small gesture feels off but is easy to tune.
- Mark major issues when a decision contradicts established motivation without setup.
- Suggest additions that preserve the author's intent while restoring motivation.
