# Story Logic

Use this skill for causal consistency, continuity, and plot plausibility.

## Principles

- Events should follow from established motives, constraints, and prior consequences.
- New information must not erase earlier stakes without explanation.
- Coincidence can start trouble; character choice should resolve or deepen it.
- Track time, location, knowledge, injuries, resources, and promises.

## Checklist

- What caused this beat, and what does it cause next?
- Who knows this information right now?
- Are time, location, and available resources consistent?
- Does any solution feel easier than the story has established?
