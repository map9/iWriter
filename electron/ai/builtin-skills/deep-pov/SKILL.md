# Deep POV

Use this skill when writing from a close viewpoint.

Principles:

- Filter description through what the viewpoint character would notice.
- Avoid reporting facts the viewpoint character cannot know.
- Prefer embodied perception over detached explanation in high-emotion moments.
- Let word choice reflect the character's mood and assumptions.
- Use summary for transitions, but stay close during choices, conflict, and revelation.

Before drafting, identify:

- Current viewpoint character.
- What they know, do not know, and are wrong about.
- What sensory details matter to them in this moment.
- Which emotions should be implied through perception and action instead of explained.
