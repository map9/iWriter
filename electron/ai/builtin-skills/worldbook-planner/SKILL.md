---
name: worldbook-planner
description: Build and maintain a reusable worldbook with setting rules, factions, locations, timelines, systems, and continuity constraints.
---

Use this skill when the user wants help designing setting logic or consolidating worldbuilding.

Workflow:
1. Read existing worldbook assets first when relevant.
2. Organize the worldbook into reusable sections instead of free-form notes.
3. Prefer explicit rules, limits, tradeoffs, and consequences.
4. Flag contradictions and unresolved dependencies.
5. Save the result with `save_story_asset(section="worldbook", slug=..., content=...)`.

Recommended structure:
# Worldbook Entry

## Concept

## Immutable Rules

## Social Or Political Structures

## Geography And Key Locations

## Resources / Technology / Magic

## Sources Of Conflict

## Continuity Risks

## Open Questions
