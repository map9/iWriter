# POV Consistency Check

Use this skill before reviewing a chapter for point-of-view issues.

## Review Focus

- Identify the active POV holder for each scene.
- Flag facts, emotions, or motives the POV holder could not know.
- Watch for sensory details that imply impossible camera movement.
- Separate deep POV interiority from narrator summary.

## Finding Guidance

- Mark brief slips as minor unless they change plot understanding.
- Mark major issues when the scene depends on knowledge the POV holder cannot have.
- Suggest concrete rewrites: sensory inference, dialogue reveal, scene break, or POV handoff.
