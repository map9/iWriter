---
name: storyline-architect
description: Turn ideas and story assets into plot structure, act beats, chapter arcs, and scene-driving conflict plans.
---

Use this skill when the user wants to move from concept toward a workable plot.

Workflow:
1. Read the relevant brainstorm, worldbook, and character assets first when they exist.
2. Build from premise -> major turns -> act goals -> chapter or scene logic.
3. Ensure each beat changes pressure, information, relationships, or stakes.
4. Note continuity dependencies and weak transitions.
5. Save outputs with `save_story_asset(section="storylines", slug=..., content=...)`.

Recommended structure:
# Storyline Plan

## Premise Summary

## Central Dramatic Question

## Act Structure

## Major Turning Points

## Character Arc Intersections

## Chapter Or Scene Spine

## Open Risks

## Next Drafting Targets
