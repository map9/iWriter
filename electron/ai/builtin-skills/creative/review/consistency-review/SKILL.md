# consistency-review

This is the **对不对** lens of the reviewer — an audit of correctness, not taste (that is `editorial-review`'s job). Two stages, each producing its own verdict; never merge them into one vague conclusion.

Ground everything in the **confirmed** chapter-outline (and any approved writing plan) — that is the comparison baseline. If the outline's `status` is not confirmed, say so rather than inventing a baseline.

## Stage 1 — fidelity (忠实度)

Is the prose faithful to what was authorized to be written?

- Does each scene deliver the outline's **goal → conflict → result** (no "顺利达成" where the outline demanded real conflict)?
- Do characters act from their **psychology triangle** (desire / fear / false belief) as set in the confirmed characters, rather than being moved around for plot convenience?
- Did the prose stay inside the declared scope — nothing invented or reordered beyond the plan?

## Stage 2 — quality/consistency (质量·硬一致)

Coarse dimensions — cover them, but write only what actually matters for this draft; a dimension that's fine needs a sentence at most:

- **Naturalness / common sense** — anything that violates基本 plausibility given the established setting.
- **POV** — nothing the POV character couldn't know; no unmarked head-hopping.
- **Character-behavior consistency** — actions consistent with who these people have been established to be.
- **Timeline consistency** (FR-5.1) — event order, elapsed time, ages, season/time-of-day markers self-consistent across chapters and not contradicting confirmed setting (`worldbuilding` history-timeline) or previously narrated facts. Check against the relevant objects and prior text **on demand** — do not build or maintain a persistent timeline table.
- **Foreshadowing** — planted hints get paid off; payoffs have a plant.
- **Style consistency** — if a `styles/{slug}.md` is active, does the prose hold that voice.

## Deliver

Per issue, fixed format: **问题描述 / 等级（高·中·低）/ 依据对象 / 建议**. Keep the Stage-1 fidelity verdict and the Stage-2 quality verdict as **two separate blocks**.

When the input is external reader feedback (试读意见), map it into the same graded format against objects/chapters — do not adjudicate whether the feedback itself is right; that is the author's call.

## Red lines

Correctness, not taste — leave "好不好" to `editorial-review`. Organize issues; do not adjudicate story decisions for the author (those go to `open-questions`, not here). Read only — the reviewer never edits the prose.
