# Character Voice — by example

Writing dialogue, interiority, or close narration so a character is recognizable without dialogue tags.

## What voice is built from

- **Vocabulary** — the words this character naturally chooses.
- **Rhythm** — clipped, ornate, hesitant, evasive, direct.
- **Attention** — what they notice first.
- **Deflection** — what they refuse to say directly.
- **Pressure response** — how the voice changes under stress.

Make each important character sound distinguishable *without* leaning on "……阿坤说" / "……老周说". If a confirmed `styles/` voice trait exists, follow it unless the scene deliberately shows a change.

正例（two voices, tags removed, still distinguishable）：
> "这事我不掺和。码头上的人都知道，我不掺和。"
> "知道。所以我没找别人。"

The first speaker repeats himself to build a wall; the second answers in four words and turns the wall into a reason. You can tell who is who without tags.

## When reviewing

Flag only *meaningful* voice drift: a line anyone could have said, or wording that contradicts the character's established temperament. Do not police every sentence for flavor.
