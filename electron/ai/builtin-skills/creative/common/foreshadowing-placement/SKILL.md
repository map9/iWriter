# Foreshadowing Placement

Use this skill to plant future payoffs without announcing them.

## Plant Types

- Object: a tool, gift, document, wound, habit, or place that can matter later.
- Gesture: a repeated behavior that later changes meaning.
- Line drop: a casual sentence that becomes relevant after new information.
- Missing detail: something absent that the reader can later reinterpret.
- Open question echo: a small reminder of an unresolved StoryBible question.

## Placement Rules

- Embed the plant inside active behavior, dialogue, or environment.
- Do not explain its future importance.
- Keep it useful in the current scene even if the later payoff never happens.
- Prefer ch+2 or later payoffs; immediate setup-payoff often feels mechanical.

## Chapter-End Check

After drafting, ask:

- Is there one detail here that can return later under pressure?
- Does StoryBible `## Open Questions` contain something this chapter can quietly prepare?
- Did this chapter acknowledge any earlier plant that should not be forgotten?

## Avoid

- Ominous author commentary.
- Too many plants in one chapter.
- Planting new mysteries during a climax that should be paying off old ones.
