# Dialogue Craft — by example

Dialogue-heavy scenes where each exchange reveals desire, conceals fear, and shifts power.

## What strong dialogue does

Beyond exchanging information: it reveals what a speaker wants, conceals what they fear, escalates conflict, or shifts power. Give each speaker a distinct rhythm, vocabulary, and pressure point. Prefer subtext when characters have reason to hide what they mean. Break long exchanges with an action beat *only* when it changes the emotional or tactical position — not to mark who's talking.

反例（information transfer）：
> "照片是谁给你的？" 阿坤问。
> "是老陈给我的，他昨天来找过我。" 老周说。

正例（desire, fear, power shift）：
> "照片是谁给你的？"
> 老周把茶杯转了半圈，没喝。"你还是别问了。"
> "我问了。"
> "你问了，就得还我一个人情。"茶杯停下来。"你现在还欠得起吗？"

The literal topic is the photo's source; the real exchange is who owes whom, and the last line changes the scene's direction.

## Quick tests

- Who wants what in this exchange, and what is each speaker avoiding saying?
- Does the final line change the scene's direction?
- Could an explanatory line become action, silence, interruption, or implication instead?
