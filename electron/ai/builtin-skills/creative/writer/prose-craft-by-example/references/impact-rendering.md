# Impact — by example

What a person does in the seconds after the ground moves: a shock, a reversal, a blow, an irreversible sentence. The failure this reference fixes is prose that skips the reaction and cuts straight to composure — technically clean, emotionally absent. Restraint says "don't over-write"; it never said "don't write it at all". A big moment that lands in two lines of dialogue was not restrained, it was skipped.

## The body arrives before the understanding

Under shock, comprehension is late. Something physical happens first, then the mind reaches for a handle — usually the wrong one, or a mundane one — and only then does the specific fear land.

反例（跳过反应）："那是我。不是像，是我。我们对视了几秒。"
正例："那是我。……我的腿在抖，不知道抖了多久。胃里一阵一阵地往上翻，我把它压回去。脑子里有个词浮上来，是新闻里的词，这些年它隔三差五出现在推送里，我每次都是划过去的。原来不是划过去就没事的。"

The order matters more than the length: legs, stomach, then the word. Reverse it and it reads as analysis.

## The fear has to be *this* person's

A generic body (racing heart, cold sweat, ringing ears) is a placeholder. What makes a reaction belong to someone is what they reach for and what they stand to lose — their职业、their position, the thing they have been protecting for years.

反例（通用）："我浑身发冷，脑子一片空白，害怕极了。"
正例："我低头看自己：T恤领口撕到肩膀以下，光着脚，脚趾在流血。我忽然想到的不是他是谁，是要是这时候有人推门进来——我还怎么在那栋楼里坐着开会。"

The second sentence is not braver writing; it is the same beat routed through a man whose whole life is his standing.

## Say the wrong thing

People under shock rarely produce the line the scene needs. They say something useless, ask a question that has no answer, or reach for an authority that does not apply here — and hear how wrong it sounds a half-second later.

反例（对位工整）："「你是谁？」我们同时问。同一个声音，同一个调。"
正例："「你——」我开口。他同时说：「这是我家。」两句话撞在一起，谁也听不清谁的。我们都闭了嘴。"

反例："「你们不能这样，我们要请律师。」"
正例："「这不合法。」我说。说出口我就知道这话说错了——不是错在道理上，是错在对象上。"

Two people speaking over each other is what actually happens; a matched-pair line is choreography. (Note the second pair still uses one antithesis — that construction is capped; spend it here, not on scenery.)

## Then stop

Give the moment its weight and leave. The reaction is two or three beats, not a page: the body, the wrong handle, the specific loss. Extending it into a set-piece is the opposite failure, and `restraint` is the counterweight — read both when a scene turns.

## The test

Cover the character's name and job. If the reaction would fit anyone in that room, it is a placeholder — rebuild it from what this person is about to lose.
